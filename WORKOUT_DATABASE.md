## 3. Exercise Mechanics & Biomechanical Reference (Core Catalog)

### Exercise 1: Barbell Flat Bench Press
* **ID:** `ex_bb_bench_press`
* **Primary Muscle:** Pectoralis Major (Sternal Head)
* **Secondary Muscles:** Anterior Deltoid, Triceps Brachii, Serratus Anterior
* **Equipment:** Standard Olympic Barbell & Flat Bench
* **Mechanics:** Compound / Horizontal Push
* **Setup Instructions:**
  1. Lie flat with eyes directly beneath the racked bar. Five points of contact: head, upper back, buttocks on bench; both feet flat on the floor.
  2. Retract and depress scapulae ("pinch shoulder blades into your back pockets").
  3. Grip bar slightly wider than shoulder-width with a full overhand thumb-around grip.
* **Execution Cues:**
  1. Unrack bar with elbows locked; stabilize bar directly over shoulders.
  2. Inhale deeply, brace abdominal core using the Valsalva maneuver.
  3. Lower bar in a controlled path touching the lower sternum / nipple line, keeping elbows tucked at ~45° to 60° relative to torso.
  4. Press barbell explosively upward and slightly backward, ending over shoulder joints. Lock elbows without protracting shoulders.
* **Common Mistakes:** Bouncing bar off the ribcage; flaring elbows out 90° (impinges rotator cuff); lifting hips off the bench.

---

### Exercise 2: Conventional Barbell Deadlift
* **ID:** `ex_bb_deadlift`
* **Primary Muscle:** Erector Spinae, Gluteus Maximus, Hamstrings
* **Secondary Muscles:** Latissimus Dorsi, Quadriceps, Forearms / Grip, Trapezius
* **Equipment:** Olympic Barbell & Weight Plates
* **Mechanics:** Compound / Hip Hinge
* **Setup Instructions:**
  1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, barbell cutting directly over mid-foot (1 inch from shins).
  2. Hinge at hips to grip the bar just outside knees with a double-overhand or hook grip.
  3. Bring shins forward until they touch the bar without moving the bar forward.
  4. Pull chest up, pack lats ("bend the bar around your shins"), creating full bodily tension before pulling.
* **Execution Cues:**
  1. Drive feet through the floor as if leg pressing the earth away.
  2. Keep bar in continuous contact with shins and thighs throughout the ascent.
  3. Lock hips and knees simultaneously at the top; stand tall without hyperextending lower back.
  4. Return bar by hinging hips back until plates touch the ground; reset tension before next rep.
* **Common Mistakes:** Rounding the lumbar spine; letting bar drift away from shins; hitching bar up thighs.

---

### Exercise 3: Barbell Back Squat (High Bar)
* **ID:** `ex_bb_squat`
* **Primary Muscle:** Quadriceps Femoris (Rectus Femoris, Vastus Lateralis/Medialis)
* **Secondary Muscles:** Gluteus Maximus, Adductor Magnus, Soleus, Core
* **Equipment:** Power Rack & Olympic Barbell
* **Mechanics:** Compound / Knee-Dominant
* **Setup Instructions:**
  1. Rest bar securely across upper trapezius muscles. Grip bar firmly with thumbs over or around.
  2. Step back with 2 to 3 deliberate steps. Set stance shoulder-width apart with toes angled out 15°–30°.
  3. Inhale, expand ribcage 360°, brace core tightly.
* **Execution Cues:**
  1. Break at hips and knees simultaneously. Push knees outward tracking in line with toes.
  2. Descend smoothly until hip crease drops below the top of the patella (parallel or deeper).
  3. Maintain upright torso angle; drive upwards through mid-foot to starting position.
* **Common Mistakes:** Knee valgus (knees caving inward); shifting weight onto toes; excessive forward torso lean.

---

### Exercise 4: Standing Overhead Barbell Press (OHP)
* **ID:** `ex_bb_ohp`
* **Primary Muscle:** Anterior Deltoid, Lateral Deltoid
* **Secondary Muscles:** Triceps Brachii, Upper Pectoralis, Core, Trapezius
* **Equipment:** Olympic Barbell
* **Mechanics:** Compound / Vertical Push
* **Setup Instructions:**
  1. Set bar in rack at collarbone height. Grip bar shoulder-width apart, elbows slightly in front of bar.
  2. Unrack, step back, squeeze glutes and brace core.
* **Execution Cues:**
  1. Tilt head slightly backward to clear chin path.
  2. Press bar vertically in a straight line. Once bar passes forehead, push head forward into neutral alignment ("peek through the window").
  3. Lock elbows at top, shrugging shoulders slightly up into upper traps.
* **Common Mistakes:** Leaning backward excessively (turning it into a standing incline bench press); unstable foot stance.

---

### Exercise 5: Barbell Bent-Over Row
* **ID:** `ex_bb_bent_row`
* **Primary Muscle:** Latissimus Dorsi, Rhomboids, Middle Trapezius
* **Secondary Muscles:** Posterior Deltoid, Biceps Brachii, Spinal Erectors
* **Equipment:** Olympic Barbell
* **Mechanics:** Compound / Horizontal Pull
* **Setup Instructions:**
  1. Stand hip-width apart. Deadlift bar to standing, then hinge hips back to a 45° torso angle.
  2. Arms hang perpendicular to the floor with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder-width.
* **Execution Cues:**
  1. Initiate pull by driving elbows back toward the ceiling and pulling bar toward lower ribcage / belly button.
  2. Squeeze shoulder blades together at the apex of movement.
  3. Lower bar smoothly under control without rounding back.
* **Common Mistakes:** Using body English / jerking torso upright to heave weight; pulling to upper chest with flared elbows.
